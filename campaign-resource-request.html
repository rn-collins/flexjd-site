Campaign materials

There is no downloadable campaign packet. Here is what there is instead.

An earlier version of this site linked to campaign packet filenames that were never published in the public repository. Those links went nowhere. Rather than leave a download button that fails, or publish a working file that has not been checked, this page says plainly what exists, what does not, and how to get what you need.

What already exists, in full, free, and public

The seven campaign pages are the campaign materials. They are not summaries of a packet held somewhere else. Each one carries the framing, the figures with their sources, the campus and national organizations, the legal framework where one applies, and a dated content calendar. You can copy from them directly.

Everything on those pages is public and linkable. If you need something to hand to a section, a student group, or an administrator, send the page link. It will stay current longer than a file you downloaded in October, and if a source moves, the page gets corrected and your link still works.

What does not exist, and why it is not being invented

A branded slide deck, flyer set, or social-graphic bundle. None has been produced under this site's editorial standard. Publishing one would mean publishing design assets and copy that have not been checked for currency, rights, accessibility, or institutional-identity permissions.

A file that carries the Northeastern or NUSL marks. This is an independent student resource. University names, logos, and visual identity are controlled by the university, and a peer site cannot license them onward to you.

Anything containing student contact details or organiser to-do lists. Material of that kind has been removed from this site's live campaign pages and will not be redistributed in a packet.

What a distributable file would have to pass first

These are the four checks that stand between a working document and a public download on this site, and they are the reason a request may come back as “not yet” rather than an attachment.

Currency
Every crisis line, deadline, statistic, and organization link re-checked against the source that published it, on the date of release — not the date it was written. Campaign material ages badly and is copied widely.
Privacy
No individual's direct phone number, personal email, home institution details, or unpublished availability. No student names attached to advocacy positions without their explicit agreement.
Accessibility
Real text rather than text baked into an image, alt descriptions, colour contrast that clears AA, and a reading order that works in a screen reader. A flyer that only works as a JPEG excludes part of the audience it is for.
Institutional rights
Marks, photography, and third-party campaign artwork used only where the owner permits redistribution. Most national campaign toolkits allow reuse under conditions; those conditions travel with the file.

If you still need something specific

Email the campaign name and what you are producing — a slide, a post, a table sign, a note to your section. That is enough to answer usefully. Concretely, what you can expect:

  • Text, framing, and figures with sources: available now, from the campaign page itself. No request needed.
  • Confirmation that a specific figure or crisis line is still current: checkable on request against the original source.
  • A working draft of an unpublished asset: only if it clears the four checks above. If it does not, you will be told which one it failed rather than sent the file anyway.
  • Permission to use university marks: not something this site can grant. That request goes to the university.

Reusing a national campaign's own toolkit is usually the faster route. Several of the organizations linked from the campaign pages publish free, ready-made social kits with their own current statistics and their own permissions already settled. Where one exists, the campaign page links to it directly — that file is maintained by the people who own the numbers.
