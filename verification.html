Editorial method

Useful first. Honest about freshness.

The tracker preserves all 328 research leads, including closed cycles that help students plan for recurring opportunities.

Record states

  • Verified current: checked against the opportunity provider’s primary source with a verification and next-review date.
  • Upcoming or rolling: the provider describes the cycle that way; students must still confirm before acting.
  • Historical cycle: a past deadline or closed cycle retained for planning.
  • Needs current-source review: useful lead whose present status has not yet been reconfirmed.
  • Broken or incomplete: retained for repair, never presented as ready to apply.

Editorial rules

Published factual claims must trace to a primary source: an official Northeastern page or document for school information, and the official sponsor, employer, court, agency, competition, fellowship, or application page for an opportunity. Secondary reporting, aggregators, social posts, and search snippets may identify a lead but cannot establish verification. Relative phrases such as “tomorrow” are replaced with dates. Sponsor pages and application links are stored separately. A record is never marked verified merely because it appeared in an earlier spreadsheet. Corrections should include the record name and its primary source.

Core Northeastern program sources were reviewed August 20, 2026. The restored opportunity corpus received an earlier editorial pass July 22, 2026 and is being rechecked record by record under the stricter primary-source standard. Historical changelog language does not override a record’s current status label.
