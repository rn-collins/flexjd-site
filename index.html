Why trust this

Built by a FlexJD student, for FlexJD students

This isn't an official Northeastern publication — it's something better for a fast-moving, remote program: maintained by someone living the same calendar you are, and maintained through dated editorial passes, with corrections logged when they are made.

Who runs it
RN Collins (she/they), Class of 2029 and SBA Chair of FlexJD Interests — the seat created specifically to represent this program. Every page is signed and dated; nothing here is anonymous.
Where the facts come from
Primary sources only: official NUSL and Northeastern pages, direct outreach to program staff, and each opportunity's own posting. Claims are cited; anything unconfirmed is flagged rather than hidden.
What it isn't
Not an official NUSL or Northeastern publication, not legal or financial advice, and not a substitute for confirming time-sensitive details with the Registrar, Financial Aid, or your advisor directly. Every page says so plainly.
How it stays current
Real, dated updates — not a static document. See exactly what changed and when on the What's New log.
