Where your cohort actually stands as you begin the four-year sequence, plus the co-op requirement, key contacts, and accommodations info. Expected graduation: June 2030.

01

Where the Class of 2030 stands

Peer planning snapshot, checked August 20, 2026: this page is for the cohort expected to begin in fall 2026. Confirm the actual first day and all deadlines in Northeastern's official Registrar calendar and your enrollment communications. Northeastern's public FlexJD pages describe an 83-credit JD completed over four years; the public degree-requirements page says the degree conferred is the JD. The former “no FlexJD notation” diploma claim needs confirmation from the Registrar and is not stated as policy here.

The 2025–2028 elective projection is a dated peer planning aid. It does not certify that every listed course will be offered or available to FlexJD students, and it does not cover the cohort's full expected period of enrollment. Use the live registration system and ASA advice rather than assuming future availability.

Peer experience, not policy: students may find early alumni outreach useful, but there is no official requirement to begin in 1L. The LinkedIn Alumni Outreach Guide and newsletter archive are optional student-created tools.

02

Co-op & Experiential Requirement

NUSL's current public pages describe two experiential terms. Because this cohort's requirements may be clarified after matriculation, ASA and the Center for Co-op and Career Development must confirm the controlling cohort rule and approve qualifying experiences.

RequirementDetail
First experiential termOne semester-long co-op consisting of a field placement paired with a concurrent reflections seminar. NUSL states that each experiential term requires at least 275 hours during a non-academic semester.
Second experiential termOne additional semester-long practical experience, which may be a co-op, field placement, or approved experiential project. NUSL states that this term also requires at least 275 hours.
Total work-experience hoursNortheastern’s public pages currently conflict on whether the additional relevant-work requirement is 350 or 1,000 hours. Ask Academic and Student Affairs to identify the controlling requirement and any waiver rule for your matriculation cohort.

Primary sources: FlexJD Admissions, FlexJD Academics, and Degree Requirements. The last two public pages conflict on 1,000 versus 350 additional relevant-work hours; only ASA can identify the controlling rule for your cohort.

Disability Access Services (DAS)

Disability Access Services (DAS) is Northeastern's current office name. Use Northeastern's official DAS process for current accommodation requests; the Student Guide is peer navigation only.
