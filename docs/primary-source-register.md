# FlexJD Primary-Source Register

Reviewed: August 20, 2026

This register is the controlling source map for institutional copy. A link appearing here does not make every downstream sentence current; each material claim must match the linked primary source and carry an appropriate review date.

## Program structure and admissions

- [FlexJD admissions](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/flexjd/) — ABA-accredited part-time format; four years; 11 semesters; three required and three optional Law On Campus intensives; application information; experiential-term structure; current admissions contact.
- [FlexJD academics](https://law.northeastern.edu/academics/programs/flexjd/) — 83 credits; representative curriculum; 275-hour practical-experience minimum; published 1,000-hour relevant-work statement.
- [JD degree requirements](https://law.northeastern.edu/academics/programs/jd/degree-requirements/) — FlexJD graduation requirements and separately published 350-hour relevant-work statement. This conflicts with the academics page and must not be silently resolved by this peer site.
- [FlexJD FAQs](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/flexjd/flexjd-faqs/) — application materials; current admissions deadline; visa and bar-eligibility cautions; published technical requirements.
- [Law On Campus](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/flexjd-law-on-campus/) — required and optional intensive structure and currently published session dates.

## Money and enrollment consequences

- [FlexJD financial aid](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/flexjd/financial-aid/) — types of aid; February 15 priority filing date; scholarship conditions; refund process; financial-aid contact.
- [FlexJD tuition and budgeting](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/flexjd/tuition-and-budgeting/) — tuition, refund schedule, withdrawals, reduced course loads, direct deposit, and aid-adjustment boundaries.
- [FlexJD financial counseling](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/flexjd/financial-counseling/) — official AccessLex counseling resources for admitted and current students.
- [NUSL scholarships](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/jd/financial-aid/scholarships/) — published terms for PILS, Law/Equity/Race, Health Policy/Law, Intellectual Property/Innovation, service-program scholarships, and the Northeastern University Law Grant. A named admissions year is not stated on the page, so the tracker treats these as source-checked rather than current-cycle confirmed.
- [Public Interest Law Support](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/jd/financial-aid/public-interest-support/) — Fund for the Public Interest, co-op stipend program, PILS benefits, and Loan Deferral and Forgiveness context.
- [Loan Deferral and Forgiveness Program](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/jd/financial-aid/ldf/) — eligibility, award method, application channel, and the now-passed November 14, 2025 and May 29, 2026 deadlines.
- [Additional resources for current students](https://law.northeastern.edu/admissions/jd/financial-aid/additional-resources/) — CISP, limited Work-Study funding for co-op, and annually offered paid upper-level positions. The page does not publish current student-application deadlines.

## Verification boundaries

- Official pages can conflict or lag. Record the conflict, review date, and responsible office rather than choosing the more convenient statement.
- An opportunity is verified only against the official sponsor, employer, court, agency, competition, fellowship, or application page.
- Search snippets and automated link checks are discovery and triage tools, not primary-source verification.
