## Schema and application rule

- The live schema deliberately rejects non-null `verified_at`; therefore this audit does **not** propose that field.
- Apply only `status`, `status_reason`, and the sponsor destination in the final link cell. Preserve all historical copy and leads.
- “Official sponsor page” means the employer, court, agency, program, or competition owner—not a law-school roundup or commercial directory.
- A reachable sponsor homepage does not verify a cycle, deadline, compensation, remote format, citizenship rule, or eligibility. Those records remain `needs-review`.
- No malformed composed URL was found in the final-link field of these 146 listings. General sponsor homepages are flagged below when they do not substantiate the named opportunity.
